HOW TO PLAY

Spend a charge on a node. Energy jumps to every node within its reach, and those nodes fire too. Light up the target number of nodes before you run out of charges. Longer chains score more.

  • RELAY Standard pulse.
  • AMPLIFIER Wide pulse.
  • SPLITTER Fires three long bolts in different directions.
  • CAPACITOR Needs two hits before it fires.
  • BATTERY Gives you a charge back.

HOVER a node to preview its reach, CLICK to fire. R restarts, M toggles sound.
